# Smart Trade operations surface

Mode: Operate. User delegated design judgment and chose code-first implementation.

## THESIS

A trade register that makes persisted cases and their supplied reference records immediately inspectable.

## OWN-WORLD

Trade register and inspection ledger: white records on #f6f7f8 canvas, deep teal #172e34 navigation, #172a32 ink, #596b73 secondary text, exact monetary strings, aligned columns and understated dividing rules. Arial/Helvetica product UI type is deliberately familiar, permitted by the skill's Operate guidance. Amber marks synthetic reference information.

## STORY

Dashboard summary and product mix lead to a searchable register; opening a case leads to source metadata and related inventories. Every expected outcome and historical control record stays visibly synthetic. No processing, screening, decision or approval action is offered.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

Desktop: fixed navigation rail, topbar with Synthetic Demo Data indicator, dashboard heading and refresh, four compact KPI columns, product distribution, then case register with filters. Case route: back link, case identifier, expected outcome, source fields, source status and tabbed inventory. Mobile: top navigation, two-column KPIs, stacked filters and contained horizontally scrollable tables; case facts reflow to two columns and overview reflows to one column.
