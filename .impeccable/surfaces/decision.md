# Governed decisions and approvals

Mode: Operate. Ordinary extension of the established operations ledger, inherited seed 50423c3b. User delegated design judgment and direct code; no comp or new visual world.

## THESIS
The reviewer can distinguish a deterministic recommendation from a pending or human-approved outcome, then trace every reason and accountable action to immutable source history.

## OWN-WORLD
Preserve DESIGN.md and token sidecar: white ruled ledgers, teal actions, compact status labels, Arial, contained tables and native controls. No decorative flow graph or new design system.

## STORY
Generate a versioned recommendation → inspect documentary/risk/incomplete reasons → select a visibly fictional demo identity → resolve assigned tasks → maker/checker approval → inspect immutable audit. Unchecked optional controls stay explicit; stale evidence prevents approval and hard blocks cannot be waived.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
Selected Decision tab, explicit run/history controls and synthetic scope introduce a paired recommendation / governed outcome register. Exact source-run links and compact reason counts precede the reason ledger. Approvals & Workflow leads with demo identity, current state, required maker/checker separation and the open task register. Audit Trail is a chronological linked ledger.

## FORM
History, source citation, policy and actor controls use incumbent affordances. Task actions require native inline rationale forms and backend-provided availability; no approval modal. Evidence stacks on mobile; tables scroll inside the ledger and the selected tab remains visible. Signature interaction: inspect a reason's original source and then act under its assigned demo role, preserving the recommendation and every audit event.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
